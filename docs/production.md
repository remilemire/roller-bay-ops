# Station production and digital cutting

Production follows allocated → scheduled → cut → assembled → checked → shipped. Allocation is required before recording production; scheduling and every production milestone are independent and optional. Recording a later milestone never invents earlier completions. The headline status is the furthest recorded step. Each completion covers the whole order; partial quantities are not supported.

## Set up the stations

1. In **Stations → Manage employees**, an admin adds each employee's full name and initials. Optional account links connect an employee to an existing application user; employees do not need their own login. Names and initials need not be unique: the permanent employee ID identifies the person. Deactivate departed employees instead of deleting them.
2. Sign in on each iPad with an eligible Microsoft work account. New accounts start pending. In **Users**, an admin chooses **Change role**, selects **Production**, and assigns cutting, assembly, checking, shipping, or a combination. Assign each dedicated floor iPad only its own station; the server rejects completions for unassigned stations.
3. The production account opens **Stations**, finds the order, opens its completion form, selects every employee who completed that work, and confirms completion. Single-station accounts open their station directly, with no switching control. Accounts with multiple stations, including staff, first choose a station, which that browser then reopens; an unassigned station in the URL asks again rather than opening another. **Change station** forgets it and returns them to that choice, which clears the order filters. **Queue**, **Completed today**, and **All allocated orders** support regular work and skipped steps. Searching also finds already processed orders. Lists refresh every ten seconds. Station queues use two columns when the available content width fits two cards of at least 520px, and one column otherwise; cards pack below shorter neighbors without changing the order of the queue’s keyboard targets. Expanding or collapsing history repacks the cards. Compact cards show the order number, quantity, ship date and current station’s recorded state beside the action, with labeled notes beneath. Expand **Production details** for each stage’s employees and full completion time; unrecorded stages remain explicit even when later stages are recorded. Pagination belongs to the whole queue and uses a quiet standalone footer. The cutting worksheet link uses the standard primary button variant. When a worksheet for the allocation is awaiting review, staff, admins, and owners can choose **Review submitted worksheet** from the manual results form. Leaving unsaved measurements prompts for confirmation.

A production session is a normal authenticated application account with restricted permissions. The server checks its current role and station assignments on every request. Production accounts can view the relevant production screens, read the active employee picker, record completions, and change their personal display settings. Cutting accounts can read instructions and locations and save/submit worksheets. They cannot manage employees, users, orders, allocations, stock, or office review. Other business endpoints deny production accounts even if the screen is bypassed.

Employee selection records trusted attribution, like the old initials entry; it does not authenticate anyone. A milestone credits one or more employees, since several people may work on one order; every completion records the credited employees and the authenticated account that entered it. Recording a milestone again with exactly the same employees is a harmless confirmation; any other set is refused, and an admin corrects the attribution. Employee selection belongs to the open order's completion form and is cleared when a new form opens; it is never shared across orders. An uncertain request still retries its original employees. Manual sign-off asks for **Completed by** inside its confirmation form. A cutting sheet instead records who is working on it: beginning it asks for its **Cutters**, one or more, which can be changed until results are submitted, and submission credits whoever is listed then without asking again. After submission, including a return for correction, only an admin milestone correction changes who is credited. Names and initials are copied into history, so renaming or deactivating someone does not rewrite previous work.

## Paperless cutting

Open an order at the cutting station and choose **Begin cutting**. This captures the allocation, cutting rules, stock details, dimensions and plan as a permanent worksheet snapshot. It freezes that allocation against replanning and cancellation. The original instructions remain readable after reconciliation. An admin may abandon an unused worksheet with a reason, provided production has not been recorded and no later worksheet depends on it.

For uncut orders, **Record cutting** explains the choice between **Sign off only** (completion without measurements) and **Use worksheet** or **Continue worksheet** (instructions and measurement entry). Except when waiting for replacement cuts, submitting the current allocation’s worksheet removes the order from the cutting work queue, including when its cut timestamp is later cleared. The order remains available in **All allocated orders** and can proceed through the other stations. When the office returns submitted measurements, the order reappears in the cutting queue. If its cut is already recorded, **Correct returned worksheet** reopens the measurements and preserves the existing sign-off. Saved worksheets remain available through office review and their existing URLs. Manual completion credits the selected employees without creating a worksheet; paper results can still be recorded through allocation completion. Manual completion remains available in the station’s **Record cutting** form after a worksheet starts; the worksheet itself only offers measurement entry and submission. It preserves the worksheet and its saved progress; cutters may resume and submit measurements later. Submission preserves an existing manual completion's time and employees. A cleared cut milestone can also be recorded manually while retaining worksheet and inventory history.

The worksheet has cut checkboxes, instructions, return measurements, locations and retained-remnant entry. Its saved plan also shows allocation metadata, cutting rules, length before cutting and estimated length afterward, tube measurements, and expected reusable offcuts with dimensions, quantities and source cuts. Estimates and offcut suggestions are instructions only; actual measurements and retained pieces still require entry. **Save progress** persists incomplete inputs and checkmarks. **Review and submit results** sends finished measurements to the office and normally records the whole-order cut milestone, credited to the sheet's cutters, in the same transaction, without modifying stock. The cut timestamp is the submission time. Retries do not duplicate completion. Returned measurements and resubmissions preserve the original completion and attribution, including admin corrections or clearing; pre-existing cut records remain unchanged.

In **Stations → Review cutting results**, staff, admins, and owners can open a submitted worksheet, accept its measurements, or return it to the cutter with a reason. The review page groups each source stock item with its outcome, measurements and retained remnants. Acceptance reconciles stock, creates retained remnants and releases reservations atomically. Production timestamps stay unchanged. Admins and owners also see incomplete worksheets in the queue and can abandon an unused sheet. Only admins and owners can choose **Replace with new measurements** to resolve a discrepancy. Replacement requires verified measurements and a reason, and the original submission stays in history.

Worksheets sharing a roll are sequenced under stock locks. A cutter must submit the preceding worksheet before starting another on that roll. A roll whose submitted results report it fully consumed cannot start another worksheet until the office accepts that report, which flags the other plans on it for replanning, or returns it for correction. The office must reconcile them in physical cutting order. Stock revisions may advance automatically only through the recorded, reviewed predecessor. An unrelated stock change blocks normal review rather than silently replacing a newer balance. Review requests require a UUID idempotency key. Exact retries return the accepted sheet; a different request after review is rejected instead of claiming its measurements were applied. Duplicate reviews do not create duplicate remnants. An explicit physical resolution breaks automatic revision propagation to already captured successors: each successor must resolve its discrepancy before its historical observations can affect current inventory.

The station requires a connection. Saved progress survives reload; unsaved edits do not. Failed saves retain the open form, and leaving dirty work prompts for confirmation. The app does not provide an offline synchronization queue.

## Corrections and historical data

Milestones are recorded at the stations. The order detail's production panel shows milestone times and employee attribution, and each milestone offers admins a correction: they can correct the credited employees and time, starting from the recorded attribution, or clear an erroneous milestone, with an expected revision and reason. Later milestones remain unchanged. Completions and corrections use UUID idempotency keys and audit events in the same transaction as the effective order change.

Migrations 0030 and 0031 preserve existing records and timestamps. Historical cutting and shipping timestamps remain visible without invented employee attribution. New assembled/checked timestamps start empty, employees and worksheets start empty, and existing accounts retain their roles. Migration 0035 moves each completion's credited employee into `production_completion_employees`, one row per employee, and drops the single-employee columns; audit snapshots written before it are read as one-employee lists. Migration 0040 does the same for cutting sheets: the employee who began each sheet becomes its one row in `cutting_worksheet_employees`, and older worksheet audit snapshots read as one-cutter lists. Inventory reconciliation no longer stamps an order as cut. Shipping is recorded through production, not the work-order PATCH endpoint.

## API and ownership

All paths below have the `/api` prefix. Mutations require the configured Origin. Shared contracts live in `@roller-bay/shared/production` and `@roller-bay/shared/employees`.

| Route                                                | Purpose / access                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /production/employees`                          | Active employee picker; assigned production account or staff                                                                  |
| `GET /production/:station/orders`                    | Station queue, completed-time range or all allocated orders; assigned production account or staff                             |
| `GET /production/orders/:id/completions`             | Effective employee attribution; assigned production account or staff                                                          |
| `POST /production/:station/orders/:id/complete`      | Whole-order completion with `employeeIds` (one or more, no duplicates); UUID `Idempotency-Key`                                |
| `POST /production/:station/orders/:id/corrections`   | Admin correction with `employeeIds`/time (or both null), reason, revision and UUID key                                        |
| `GET, POST /production/cutting/orders/:id/worksheet` | Read or begin a saved sheet with its `employeeIds`; GET returns 204 when the order has no live sheet; cutting access          |
| `GET /production/cutting/worksheets/:id`             | Read saved sheet; cutting access                                                                                              |
| `PUT /production/cutting/worksheets/:id/draft`       | Save partial form and checkmarks with expected revision; cutting access                                                       |
| `PUT /production/cutting/worksheets/:id/employees`   | Change the sheet's cutters (`employeeIds`) with expected revision until results are submitted; cutting access                 |
| `POST /production/cutting/worksheets/:id/submit`     | Submit measurements and optional current draft with expected revision; credits the sheet's cutters atomically; cutting access |
| `GET /production/cutting/worksheets`                 | Staff review queue (submitted sheets); admins and owners also see incomplete sheets                                           |
| `POST /production/cutting/worksheets/:id/review`     | Staff reconciliation with UUID key and expected revision; only admins and owners may supply a resolution                      |
| `POST /production/cutting/worksheets/:id/return`     | Staff return for correction; revision and reason                                                                              |
| `POST /production/cutting/worksheets/:id/abandon`    | Admin abandon unused sheet; revision and reason                                                                               |
| `GET, POST /employees`                               | Admin employee directory and creation                                                                                         |
| `PUT /employees/:id`                                 | Admin update/deactivate; expected revision                                                                                    |

`production` owns the completion table, its credited-employee rows, attribution, corrections and station read model. Requested employee ids are sorted by the shared contract, so replay hashes and retry keys ignore the order they were ticked in. Its controller delegates to `ProductionService`; its injected repository returns records, and its presenter formats public responses. Work orders own the order and scheduling. Production updates the existing order timestamp projection through `WorkOrdersService` inside the completion transaction; no duplicate order type is introduced. The station-to-timestamp mapping lives in the work-order milestone definitions, not in a repository.

`cutting-worksheets` owns its tables, including the sheet's cutters, saved instructions, draft/submission state, sequence rules and audit changes. Its injected repository only queries that feature's table. `CuttingWorksheetsService` has no dependency on allocations, work orders or stock services. Allocations calls worksheet service guards for planning, cancellation and reconciliation; it never inspects worksheet rows itself.

`CuttingWorkflowService` coordinates beginning, submitting, abandoning and reviewing sheets through allocation, work-order, stock and worksheet services. One explicit transaction crosses those calls; its lock order is allocation, order when needed, worksheet, then stock sorted by ID. Allocations owns fabric plans and reconciliation; stock items owns stock mutation rules. Employees is a separate directory, and users remains the authentication identity.

Repositories are injected. New production/worksheet methods receive the active transaction explicitly; the existing allocation repository binds itself internally when joining a transaction. Controllers do not call repositories, and repositories do not import services or response presenters. These ownership changes move TypeScript files without renaming or recreating database tables.

## Validation

`production.integration.test.ts` uses disposable PostgreSQL/Redis fixtures and covers restricted production access, assignment checks, Origin checks, active employees, milestones credited to several employees and the same-set confirmation rule, legacy one-employee snapshots, allocation prerequisites, late/skipped stages, timestamps, historical attribution, corrections, concurrent retries, sheet freezing, draft persistence, stock sequencing, double review, abandonment and explicit discrepancy resolution. The full API suite verifies existing routes and workflows too.

`production.spec.ts` exercises station navigation, unscheduled completion, directory maintenance and a digital cutting/review workflow on desktop and tablet. It verifies saved checkmarks and measurements survive reload and dirty measurements survive background refreshes. Browser tests use intercepted API responses; they do not prove Microsoft sign-in or physical iPad behavior.

## Cancelled orders and skipped cutting results

Order cancellation, allocation cancellation and unscheduling have separate controls; see [work orders](work-orders.md#cancellation-and-scheduling-actions). Production facts remain recorded. Cancelled orders are excluded from station queues and cannot receive new production completions, while admins may still correct historical attribution. Removing a fabric plan can preserve already-recorded milestones without leaving an active allocation.

A worksheet closed with `skipped_at` is neither abandoned nor reviewed. Its saved content remains readable, but it cannot be edited, submitted, returned or reconciled and is excluded from the review queue. New cutting can continue with released stock; dependent worksheet review must explicitly resolve the missing measurement baseline. Skipping never updates stock measurements.

## Order recuts

Once the order has a fabric allocation, choose **Request recut** from its Production section, a station card, or an open cutting worksheet. A cutter who finds a flaw or runs short can request just the affected quantity and submit the actual roll measurements. Staff can open a shipped order for a customer replacement. The replacement has its own station work and sign-offs, and the original shipment date stays on the order. Select the blind from the original allocation, enter the replacement quantity and give the reason. The original order quantity and sign-offs stay intact. Outstanding requests for the same blind line can cover up to its original quantity.

**Recuts awaiting fabric** on the Overview counts individual requests that need an assignment and opens the filtered cutting queue. Staff, admins, and owners can choose **Assign fabric** on the station card. The picker shows matching rolls and remnants that fit the entire request after existing reservations, using the original cutting rules. A roll reported consumed on a submitted worksheet is excluded even while office review is pending. Each request uses one stock item. The cutting station opens a separate replacement worksheet. Its first submission records the replacement cut and sends it to assembly. If the original worksheet is submitted while replacements still need cutting, recording those measurements leaves the order’s cut sign-off pending. Submitting the last replacement records the whole-order cut with that sheet’s cutters. If staff withdraw the last outstanding request instead, the cutter can confirm completion manually. Replacements requested after a whole-order cut preserve its original sign-off. Office review, including chronological review when both sheets used the same roll, uses the normal worksheet queue. Returning measurements preserves the production sign-off for the replacement.

Assembly signs off the replacement and checking accepts it. Open recuts block new whole-order checking and shipping sign-offs. Acceptance and inventory reconciliation happen independently. Staff can withdraw a request with a reason. An unused started worksheet must first be abandoned by an admin. A physically cut replacement must have its inventory reconciled before withdrawal. Order history records requests, assignments, worksheet activity, replacement sign-offs and withdrawals.

The production API exposes the original lines at `GET /production/orders/:id/recut-blinds`, request creation at `POST /production/orders/:id/recuts`, and order history summaries at `GET /production/orders/:id/recuts`. Staff use `GET /production/recuts/:id/stock` and `POST /production/recuts/:id/assign` or `withdraw`. The cutter reads or starts the replacement worksheet at `/production/cutting/orders/:id/recut-plans/:planId/worksheet`; subsequent edits and review use its worksheet ID. Assembly and checking sign off at `POST /production/:station/recuts/:id/complete`. Recut writes use UUID idempotency keys and edits require the current recut revision.
